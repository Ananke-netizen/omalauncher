The first release of this fork: Omalauncher 0.5.0, upstream's changes since (apps from a work profile beside your own, sorting a folder's apps by dragging them), and the fork's own: a feed left of the first home page with Hacker News, X and tech and news sites, folders that open out of their tile, and pausing or turning on work apps from a work app's menu. Signed with the fork's own key, so it can't update a debug build or the Play Store's: uninstall that first, after backing up your widgets.
